## Why can this be checked without quantum hardware?

This experiment is an exact algebraic obstruction test, not a device
experiment. A full-column-rank witness modulo a prime proves full column rank
for the corresponding integer or rational matrix. Quantum hardware would be
needed for claims about noise, calibration, sampling advantage, or physical
device behavior. R200 makes none of those claims.
