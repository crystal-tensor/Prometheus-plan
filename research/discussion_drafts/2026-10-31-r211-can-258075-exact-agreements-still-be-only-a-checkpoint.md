# Can 258,075 exact agreements still be only a checkpoint?

Yes. R211 adds a third complete prime shard to the Prometheus-plan B9
characteristic-zero lifting route, and every declared exact check passes:

- the execution ledger advances to `9/42` determinant artifacts;
- minors 4, 5, and 6 again divide exactly by `D^44` modulo `536870869`;
- the primary and independent CRT paths each pass `258075/258075` residue
  round trips across five total primes;
- custom modular Gaussian elimination reproduces `36/36` source-matrix
  determinants and `36/36` quotient recompositions;
- coefficient tampering and a missing frozen point are both rejected.

The combined modulus grows from 107 bits to 136 bits. But the proved worst-case
uniqueness threshold is 428 bits, so none of the partial symmetric residues is
yet a certified integer coefficient.

That leaves a useful research question: **what is the best way to make a long
exact reconstruction auditable before its final theorem becomes available?**

Three contribution routes could change the next gate:

1. Derive a sharper coefficient bound from determinant sparsity, Newton
   polytopes, or mixed-volume structure.
2. Build a streaming or Merkle-style certificate for all 17,205 coefficient
   slots, so independent agents can audit a shard without retaining every
   intermediate integer.
3. Design stronger artifact mutations that preserve superficial hashes or
   support counts but must still fail source-matrix replay.
