# Does Leaving the XZ Plane Reveal a Hidden Quadratic Charge?

R198 closed one continuous XZ-plane Jordan-Wigner chart. R199 asks the next adversarial question: if the parity axis acquires a genuine Y component, does an exact quadratic-Majorana charge reappear?

The public contract was frozen before either unseen coupling opened. It uses

```text
A = (-4X+3Z)/5
B = (-3X-4Z)/5
P = cA+sY
Q = -sA+cY
R = B
```

with the rational projective parameterization `c=(1-t^2)/(1+t^2)` and `s=2t/(1+t^2)`.

## What survived the test?

- `4/4` exact continuous rows at `n=4,5` pass.
- `16` exact maximal-minor determinants leave zero finite real common roots.
- Every projective endpoint has full column rank.
- `432/432` frozen rational-frame rows at `n=6,8,10` have full rank under two primes.
- An independent, non-importing implementation reproduces `4/4` continuous certificates and all `432/432` grid rows under third prime `1,000,037`.
- The independent grid has zero missing, extra, or duplicate rows and `error_count=0`.
- The positive control recovers the expected 37-dimensional nullspace and all six onsite A-axis charges.

## What does this mean?

Within this one publicly frozen A-Y great-circle meridian, the declared quadratic-Majorana-plus-parity pool exposes no extra conserved charge at the tested continuous and finite-size boundaries. That is a useful negative boundary for one fermionization escape route.

It is not a full Bloch-sphere exclusion, a quartic-charge exclusion, a nonlocal-duality exclusion, a nonintegrability or chaos theorem, a hardness result, a Quantum PCP or NLTS theorem, a BQP separation, or a solved frontier. Scientific promotion remains false and new credit remains zero.
