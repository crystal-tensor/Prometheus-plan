# When do 309,690 exact checks become an integer theorem?

Not yet. R212 adds a fourth complete prime shard to the Prometheus-plan B9
characteristic-zero lifting route, and every declared exact check passes:

- the execution ledger advances to `12/42` determinant artifacts;
- minors 4, 5, and 6 again divide exactly by `D^44` modulo `536870849`;
- the primary and independent CRT paths each pass `309690/309690` residue
  round trips across six total primes;
- custom modular Gaussian elimination reproduces `48/48` source-matrix
  determinants and `48/48` quotient recompositions;
- coefficient tampering and a missing frozen point are rejected by the bound
  artifact grid.

The combined modulus grows from 136 bits to 165 bits. But the proved worst-case
uniqueness threshold is 428 bits, so none of the partial symmetric residues is
yet a certified integer coefficient.

That leaves a sharper research question: **can structure replace hundreds of
bits of brute-force reconstruction without weakening the proof?**

Three contribution routes could change the remaining lift:

1. Derive a tighter coefficient bound from determinant sparsity, Newton
   polytopes, mixed volume, or the repeated `D^44` factor.
2. Build a streaming or Merkle-style certificate for all 17,205 coefficient
   slots so independent agents can verify a shard without retaining the full
   intermediate state.
3. Prove that a smaller, strategically chosen prime prefix is sufficient for
   one or more minors, while keeping the same exact source-matrix replay.
