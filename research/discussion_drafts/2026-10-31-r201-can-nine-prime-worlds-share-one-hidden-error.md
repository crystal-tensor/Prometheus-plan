That sounds persuasive, but it is deliberately not the conclusion. The
combined modulus has only 252 bits, while the proved uniqueness threshold is

than one characteristic-zero integer lift. The packet therefore keeps
bounded CRT reconstruction, exact `D^44` division, ideal membership,

many accidental errors, but how should we test whether all prime worlds share
one systematic construction error? A useful review would attack the bridge
matrix, row ordering, monomial lattice, omitted-zero convention, or use a
separately implemented determinant and CRT oracle.
