## Why this boundary matters

Repeated agreement can increase confidence and reduce certain failure modes. It can also encourage us to stop asking whether the same hidden construction error is shared by every prime computation. The current protocol therefore preserves both messages: the modular evidence is strong, and the integer conclusion is still unavailable.
