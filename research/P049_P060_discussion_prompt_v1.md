# Which of twelve frontier problems can be falsified before it can be solved?

Problems #049–#060 span reaction dynamics, self-assembly, protein ensembles,
genotype-to-phenotype maps, cancer screening, aging, neurodegeneration, immune
control, antimicrobial resistance, pandemic warning, connectomics, and gene editing.
That breadth creates a dangerous temptation: count datasets, model parameters, or papers
as progress. This update takes the opposite test—what is the first result that could
honestly fail?

The new activation packet passes `13/13` machine checks and gives every problem an explicit
denominator, unopened-data rule, falsifier, and safety boundary:

- **#049 Exact Molecular Reaction Dynamics** — Can a reaction-dynamics solver predict an observable it was not tuned on before it asks for scale credit?
- **#050 Programmable Self-Assembling Matter** — If off-target interactions are part of the physics, can inverse design still win on structures it never saw?
- **#051 Protein Dynamics and Function Prediction** — Can an ensemble prediction explain held-out dynamics that a beautiful single structure cannot?
- **#052 Genotype-to-Phenotype Mapping** — Does a genotype-to-phenotype model survive a change of ancestry, environment, and measurement protocol?
- **#053 General Early Cancer Detection and Personalized Cure** — What evidence would distinguish an early-cancer signal from an expensive cascade of false positives?
- **#054 Mechanisms and Safe Reversal of Aging** — If lifespan moves, what would prove that function improved without merely changing how disease was counted?
- **#055 Neurodegenerative Disease Root Causes** — Can a proposed mechanism predict who changes next, not merely describe who is already ill?
- **#056 Programmable Immune Control** — Can an immune controller hit its target state without borrowing success from cytokine toxicity?
- **#057 Global Reversal of Antimicrobial Resistance** — Can an AMR forecast beat yesterday's resistance rate where surveillance is thinnest?
- **#058 Pandemic Prediction and Interdiction** — How early is an early warning after false alarms and reporting delays are charged to the score?
- **#059 Human Brain Connectome and Cognition** — Can a connectome predict cognition after family structure, head motion, and scanner artifacts are denied an escape route?
- **#060 Safe Scalable Gene Editing** — What would make an editing result fail even when its on-target number looks excellent?

Three questions for collaborators:

1. Which denominator above is still too weak to make a positive result interesting?
2. Which acceptance threshold would you tighten before any data are opened?
3. Which public dataset or independent replay team could attack one gate without expanding
   beyond #049–#060?

The boundary matters: this is an activation packet, not twelve claimed solutions. High-risk
medical and biological lanes remain protocol-only; no intervention is executed.
